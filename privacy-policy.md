# Privacy Policy

This app (“Stream Automation”) is a personal tool created and maintained by Tony Ramos to automate scheduling and management of live streams for a YouTube channel. It accesses the Google YouTube API for the following purposes:
- Scheduling and starting live streams
- Updating video titles, descriptions, and playlists

This app does not collect, store, or share any user information. All access is limited to the app owner’s Google account and channel.
